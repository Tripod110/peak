## Direction change

Peak is no longer being built as a passive-income launch. The focus now is **developing it as an
all-in-one fitness app that feels like a personal coach**. Train, Food, Sleep and Grocery are all
core.

**Parked, not dropped:** instrumentation, recruiting users, distribution, Pro pricing and Stripe,
and the roadmap's retention gates.

How we got here:

1. **Four reviews.** Separate reviews looked at the app as a new user, an experienced lifter, a
   mobile/accessibility specialist and a product strategist.
2. **Plateau engine first.** The lifter review found that the plateau engine told lifters who were
   still progressing to deload. That became the first fix.
3. **Plan rebuilt around the new direction.**
   - Coaching that's tailored to you
   - Logging anything you train
   - Lift goals with a projected outlook

## What changed today

### Merge and security (v41)
- **Upstream merged.** Pulled the 10 upstream commits (v38 and v40) and merged the local work that
  hadn't been committed.
- **Backups no longer carry the device ID.** They don't export `deviceId`, and a restore never
  overwrites the device's own ID.
- **Worker hardening:**
  - push endpoints are limited to an allowlist of real push services
  - subscription keys are shape-checked
  - new subscriptions are capped per day
  - the global scan cap is reserved *before* each Gemini call
  - requests with no Origin header are rejected

### Plateau engine fixes (v41, D-21)
- **Reps at a load count as progress.** Rows 8/6/6 → 8/8/7 used to get "deload to 140".
  High-rep work past the 12-rep cap counts too.
- **"Climbing" is judged locally.** A lift rebuilding after a short break is no longer deloaded.
- **Heavy and light days are separate.** Each rep scheme builds from its own last session.
- **Heavy singles don't set the deload ceiling.**
- **An extra set can't block a weight increase.**
- **Smaller jumps:** about 2.5% for every lift (lower-body lifts used to jump 5%).
- **The plateau card no longer contradicts itself.** It reads the routine, not the last 7 days.

### The Coach (v42, D-22)
- **A coach card on Today.** It reads the last month and picks one state:
  - getting started
  - on a roll
  - grinding (*switch it up*)
  - holding strength on a cut
  - drifting
  - run down
  - coming back from a break
- **"Switch it up":** variation swaps, rep-range changes, a lighter week, or a new split. Each is
  one tap and undoable.
- **Coach voice setting:** Encouraging, Straight-talking or Drill sergeant. The advice is the same
  in all three; only the wording changes.
- **Post-workout debrief:** a plain-words verdict per lift, e.g. "+1 rep at 185 lb, that's
  progress".
- **Lighter-week mode:** 7 days at about 10% less weight with a set less. Progression resumes from
  the last real session afterwards.
- **Weekly check-in:** sessions against plan, PRs, sleep, protein and goal pace. "Got it" hides it
  until next week.
- **Optional Gemini wording** of the check-in. It's off by default and uses your own key only. Any
  reply that mentions a number or lift not in the facts is rejected.
- **A coach line on the Train tab.**

### Lift goals and outlook (v42)
- **Goals on any lift.** Set a weight × reps goal, optionally by a date, from "Why this target?".
- **Projection chart.** It projects your trend forward using Theil–Sen (a trend fit that one freak
  session can't swing) and gives a likely date range, plus whether you're ahead of or behind your
  target date.
- **No invented dates.** When a lift is flat or too new, the chart says so.

### Log anything (v43, D-23, D-24)
- **Custom exercises.** Set the type, per-hand, loading, default sets × reps and muscles. Your
  definition overrides every guess Peak makes from the name.
- **Rename a lift everywhere,** with a one-step undo.
- **Any activity.** "Log cardio" is now "Log activity": you can name your own activities, and they
  get chips next time. Optional distance and notes.
- **Several routines.** New routine (blank or copied from a split), switch between them, rename,
  delete. Onboarding has "Build my own".
- **Save any logged session as a routine day.**

## In progress (uncommitted)

The training-engine refinements are written but **not yet tested or committed**:

- **Re-deload grace:** two attempts at the old top weight after a deload before calling it
  stalled again.
- **Back-off set type:** progression judges only the top sets.
- **Rep and effort check in the rest dock:** after a pre-filled set is ticked, the dock asks "All 5
  reps?" (with quick −1/−2 fixes) and offers Easy/Hard. When every top set is easy, the next
  increase is double.
- **MRV warning:** the plateau note flags routines that program more volume than you can recover
  from.
- **Side delts:** now a muscle of their own, separate from front and rear delts.
